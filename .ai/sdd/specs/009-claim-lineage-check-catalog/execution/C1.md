### Mental Model and Execution Order

- **Implemented** — A deterministic result first receives one stable
  discriminator from its existing `Signal.id`; changing condition or finding
  copy does not change that identity.
  Source: `src/lib/check-catalog.ts`.
- **Implemented** — App records that discriminator in the seven-field
  metadata-only snapshot. History selection requires scope + asset + check +
  discriminator, so same-family siblings never alternate in one stream.
  Source: `src/App.tsx`, `src/lib/check-history-store.ts`, `src/types.ts`.
- **Implemented** — Legacy six-field rows are invalid for the current shape
  and are filtered before current/previous comparison, dedupe, or retention.
  They can neither become current nor previous for a discriminator-bearing
  result. Source: `src/lib/check-history-store.ts`.
- **Implemented** — Claim lineage compares explicit result references only
  with exact envelope IDs. Shared asset identity and configured asset
  evidence bindings are insufficient. Source: `src/lib/claim-lineage.ts`.
- **Implemented** — One source node may own multiple ordered outgoing edges,
  and the renderer presents each one without lossy keying. Source:
  `src/components/ClaimLineageInspector.tsx`.
